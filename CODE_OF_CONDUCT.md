# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, welcome newcomers, and keep criticism about the
work, not the person. Harassment, personal attacks, and publishing others' private
information are not tolerated.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainers through a
[private security advisory](https://github.com/Blackrose-blackhat/MorningContext/security/advisories/new)
(it reaches maintainers only) or by contacting a maintainer directly on GitHub. All reports are
reviewed and handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions, and
temporarily or permanently ban contributors, for behavior they deem inappropriate.
